Weijie Dai    戴炜杰

I am a PhD student at Tsinghua SIGS. I work on optical turbulence, free-space optics, and optical wireless communications.

Previously, I received my M.E. from the same department and my B.S. from University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). Before my M.E., I spent a year as a Research Assistant at UESTC working on electron field emission.

Please feel free to contact me if you are interested in my research.
